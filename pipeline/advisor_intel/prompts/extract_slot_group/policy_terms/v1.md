Policy terms: waiting periods, benefit terms, definitions and escalations of existing policies.
- Waiting periods for income protection are in months ("a three-month waiting period"; "it pays after a month").
- "It pays until sixty-five" is the benefit-to-age; a family income benefit pays for a number of years.
- Disability definitions: own occupation, suited occupation, any occupation, functional impairment, activities of daily living.
- Accelerated benefits reduce the life cover when they pay out ("it comes off the life cover").
- Premium and cover escalation are yearly rates ("it goes up ten percent a year").
