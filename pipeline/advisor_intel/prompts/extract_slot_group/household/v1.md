Household: marital status, marital regime, whether the spouse is in the plan, and the date of marriage.
- Married "in community of property" is icop. An antenuptial contract (ANC) is anc-accrual only when accrual is mentioned or confirmed; an ANC with no word about accrual gives two hypotheses (anc-accrual and anc-no-accrual).
- Customary marriages and marriages concluded abroad are their own regimes.
- "We live together" is a life partner, not married.
- The spouse is in the plan when both are being advised together or the adviser says they will include the partner.
