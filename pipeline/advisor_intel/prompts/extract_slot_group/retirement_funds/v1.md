Retirement funds: pension, provident and preservation funds, retirement annuities (RAs), and living or life annuities in payment.
- "My company fund" is a pension or provident fund: if the client doesn't say which, give both as hypotheses.
- An RA is a retirement annuity. A living annuity pays an income the client chooses (2.5%–17.5% of the capital a year); a life (guaranteed) annuity pays a fixed income.
- The two-pot savings component is part of the fund value, not a separate fund.
- Owner: "my RA" said by the spouse belongs to the spouse.
- Contributions: separate the member's and the employer's contributions when the client distinguishes them; "seven and a half percent of my salary" is a RATE for the client to resolve against salary — quote it as said.
