Events: uncertain future events with a cash impact that the forecast should model — study overseas, an inheritance, selling a property or business, emigrating, a large purchase, supporting family, a health event.
- The likelihood is in the client's words ("she might", "we'll definitely sell") as a PROBABILITY_EVENT.
- Timing may be a year or a duration from the call.
- A cost is negative and an inflow positive; keep foreign currencies.
