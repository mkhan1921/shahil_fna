Engagement: why the client came, what they want, what worries them, and their other advisers — in the client's own words.
- Quote the client; keep each answer to one or two short sentences of their words.
- Objectives are what they want ("retire at sixty without worrying"); concerns are what they fear ("if something happens to me, how will my wife cope").
- Other advisers are named professionals (an attorney, an accountant, another planner).
