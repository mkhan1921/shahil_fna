- School fees are often said per month or per term: quote the period as said; code converts to annual.
- "She might go overseas to study" is a PROBABILITY_EVENT for tertiary (and the cost may be in pounds, euros or dollars: keep the currency).
- "Straight after matric" means tertiary starts at 18; "after a gap year" means 19.
- A degree's length: three years for most BA/BCom/BSc, four for engineering, law (LLB) and many professional degrees, six for medicine — but only use what the client says or clearly implies.
- Costs said for a future year ("it'll be about R200,000 a year by then") keep the year in the interpretation; code converts to today's money.
- Savings for education include unit trusts or endowments the client says are "for the kids' varsity".
