Expenses: the household's monthly living costs, classified into the FNA expense catalog.
- Pick the catalog line for each budget item (the catalog list and hints are in the context). Anything that fits no line is a custom expense with a category.
- Bond and vehicle repayments, life and risk premiums, and retirement or investment contributions are not living expenses: they belong to liabilities, policies and funds.
- Quote the period as said ("R12,000 a year for the car licence and service"); code converts to monthly.
- Medical aid deducted from salary is income-side; medical aid paid by debit order is an expense.
- "Groceries are about eight to ten a month" is a range; slang amounts ("a bar", "two bar") are thousands.
