Income changes: expected changes to a person's income that the forecast should model — a promotion, a retrenchment risk, going part-time, a new job, a business sale.
- The likelihood words matter ("I might be retrenched", "I'll definitely get the promotion"): return them in a PROBABILITY_EVENT.
- The new income is often relative ("half of what I earn now"): use AMOUNT_RELATIVE against the person's gross monthly income.
- The year may be relative ("in two years"): use DURATION_FROM_ANCHOR from the call.
