Policies: life, lump-sum disability, income protection, severe illness, funeral and family income cover.
- Separate lump-sum cover (life, disability, severe illness, funeral) from a monthly benefit (income protection, family income benefit).
- Group cover through the employer is isGroup; it usually ends when the person leaves the job.
- The life assured is whose life the policy covers, which may differ from who pays.
- Beneficiary: "it pays to my wife" is spouse; "into a trust for the kids" is trust; no nomination means the estate.
- Insurer names: use the registry and the provider list; never guess an insurer that was not said.
