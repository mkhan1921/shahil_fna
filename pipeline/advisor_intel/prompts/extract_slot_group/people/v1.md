People: each person's date of birth, how they earn their income, smoking, occupation, employer, health and hazardous pursuits.
- A stated age ("I'm fifty-two", "I turn forty in March") is an AGE form for the date-of-birth slot; code turns it into a date of birth. Never compute a year yourself.
- Employment type: someone who owns their business and draws a salary from it is self-employed; commission earners and contractors are their own types; "I'm on pension" is retired.
- Health notes: quote the condition as said; never infer a diagnosis from a medication or a symptom.
- Hazardous pursuits include scuba diving, motor racing, private flying, mountaineering and travel to high-risk countries.
- The spouse's facts belong to the spouse even when the client says them ("my wife smokes").
