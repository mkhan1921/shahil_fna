Estate: wills, executors, guardians for minor children, trusts and funeral cost.
- "We did wills years ago, I think" is yes with a certainty word; "I'm not sure we ever signed it" is unsure.
- A guardian nomination only matters with minor children; with none it is not applicable.
- Executor: the person or firm named ("the bank", "my brother").
- A trust planned in the will (testamentary trust) counts as a trust.
