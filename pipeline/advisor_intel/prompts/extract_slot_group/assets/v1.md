Assets: property, vehicles, cash, investments, business interests, and what each is earmarked for.
- A tax-free savings account (TFSA) is its own type; an endowment or sinking fund is endowment; offshore investments are offshore even when held through a local platform.
- Foreign-currency values keep their currency ("about forty thousand dollars").
- Earmark: "that's for the kids' varsity" is education; "our rainy-day money" is emergency; "that's our retirement" is retirement.
- Owner: "our house" is joint; "my car" said by the spouse is the spouse's.
- Base cost is what they paid, only when said ("we bought it for one point two in 2015").
