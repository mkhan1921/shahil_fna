Estate needs: extra capital or income the family would need on death, cash bequests, the accrual starting value and ported abatement.
- Extra income on death covers costs that would appear only then (childcare, help at home).
- Extra capital covers lump sums such as business debts or a bequest to charity.
- Accrual commencement value only applies to an ANC with accrual: the person's net estate when they married.
- Ported abatement only exists when a previous spouse died and part of their estate duty abatement was unused.
