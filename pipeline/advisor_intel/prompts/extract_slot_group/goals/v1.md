Goals: other things the client is saving for — travel, a car, a deposit, a wedding, home improvements, a sabbatical.
- Each goal is its own object with a cost, a target year, savings so far and a monthly contribution.
- Costs said for a future date keep the date in the interpretation; code converts to today's money.
- "In three years" is a duration from the call; "for my fiftieth" relates to the person's age.
- Priority only when the client signals it ("that's the big one", "nice to have").
