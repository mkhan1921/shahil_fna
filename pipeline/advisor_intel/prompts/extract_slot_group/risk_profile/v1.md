Risk profile: the eight investor risk questions (tolerance and capacity).
- Choose the option label the client's words support; a hedge ("I suppose", "fairly") is the certainty word.
- Don't answer a question from the adviser's description of the client; it must come from the client's words or clear behaviour they describe ("in 2020 I sold everything" supports the drawdown question).
- Capacity questions (horizon, emergency fund, income stability) can be implied from other facts: if not discussed, leave them not_discussed — code derives them.
