Income: each person's salary, bonus, other income and payroll deductions.
- Keep the basis as said: "I take home forty-two" is net; "my package is a million a year" is gross annual; "I earn sixty" with nothing else is basis unknown.
- A 13th cheque is a bonus equal to one month's salary: return it as said ("a thirteenth cheque") with the amount if one is given.
- Commission, rental and freelance income go in other taxable income; maintenance received and income protection benefits in non-taxable income.
- Medical aid via the payslip and the number of people on the scheme are separate slots.
- For business owners, "the business is growing a bit" belongs to the business growth rate slot as a vague RATE.
