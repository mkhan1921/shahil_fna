Dependants: children, parents, siblings and others the household supports.
- A school grade gives an age: grade + 6 ("in grade nine" is fifteen); matric is eighteen. Return the age as an AGE form for the date-of-birth slot.
- Parents or siblings who receive money each month are dependants with monthly support and a number of years.
- "Until she's done with varsity" for dependency end is implied from the study plan, not a separate number.
- Special needs means lifelong or long-term dependency; a learning difficulty alone is not special needs unless the client says it means lasting support.
