Retirement: when each person wants to stop full-time work, the income they want then, and other retirement income.
- "In about eight years" is a duration from the call; code adds it to the person's age.
- "Sixty, or maybe sixty-five if we need to" is two hypotheses, with the client's own weighting if they gave one.
- An income goal as a share of salary ("seventy percent of what I earn") is percent mode; as an amount or as "about the same as now / a bit less" it is amount mode, with the relative form against current spending.
- Adviser suggestions ("most people your age plan for sixty-five") are not the client's goal unless the client agrees.
- The planning age is how long the money must last ("plan for us living to ninety-five").
